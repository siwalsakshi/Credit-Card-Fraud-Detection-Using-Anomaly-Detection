System Design

End-to-End Pipeline & Architecture

Data Flow Diagram (Level 0) and decoupled 4-layer AI architecture powering real-time inference.

01

Ingestion & Token Masking

5,000 raw transaction stream ingested with PCI-DSS CARD_XXXX#### tokenization, privacy masking, and validation.

02

Feature Engineering & SMOTE

Cyclic diurnal hours (sin/cos), log-scaled purchase values, categorical one-hot vectors, and SMOTE augmentation.

03

Multi-Model Anomaly Scoring

Concurrent evaluation across Isolation Forest, LOF, PyTorch Autoencoder MSE, and One-Class SVM boundaries.

04

Demographic Fairness & Alert

Automated False Positive Rate monitoring across card types, sub-2ms response routing, and bank ledger updates.
