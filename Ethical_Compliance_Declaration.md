## 2. Ethical Compliance Commitments

As the developer and author of the Credit Card Fraud Detection solution, I hereby solemnly declare and affirm that:

1. **Financial Access Protection:**
   - I recognize that fraud classification systems govern individuals' real-time access to financial liquidity and credit.
   - The models developed in this project actively penalize False Positives to ensure that legitimate transactions by law-abiding citizens are not unduly declined or blocked.

2. **Fairness Across Demographic Groups & Card Networks:**
   - I have conducted an automated **Fairness Audit** inspecting False Positive Rates (FPR) across diverse payment networks (`Visa`, `MasterCard`, `American Express`, `Discover`, `RuPay`).
   - The feature engineering pipeline explicitly excludes sensitive socio-demographic indicators (such as postal code proxies, nationality, gender, or religion) to prevent algorithmic discrimination.

3. **Data Privacy & PCI-DSS Alignment:**
   - All Primary Account Numbers (PANs) and credit card identifiers utilized in this project are strictly masked using industry standard format (`BIN-XXXX-XXXX-LAST4`), ensuring that no real Personally Identifiable Financial Information (PIFI) is exposed or unencrypted.
   - The empirical transaction dataset respects data protection mandates and anonymity standards.

4. **Transparent Anomaly Scoring & Contestability:**
   - The anomaly detection models (Isolation Forest, Local Outlier Factor, Neural Autoencoder, and One-Class SVM) output continuous anomaly scores rather than opaque hard binary rejections.
   - System design recommendations mandate dynamic step-up verification (e.g., Two-Factor SMS/App OTP challenge) rather than instantaneous account termination upon anomaly detection.

5. **Academic & Professional Integrity:**
   - All empirical analyses, synthetic SMOTE algorithms, exploratory visualisations, and code implementations represent genuine, authentic engineering work conducted in accordance with the HCLTech Career Shaper Programme Guidelines.
